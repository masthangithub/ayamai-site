Two open questions about how machines might come to understand.

AYAMAI (pronounced “I am AI”) is a small, open research initiative. It hosts two independent projects, each at an early stage. We share the ideas, the code and the doubts to find researchers who want to explore them with us.

DREAMS

Can a machine keep learning without forgetting what it knew?

Data Relations, Entropy, And Mind Simulations. A study of continual learning and reflective loops: experiences encoded as data, linked into memory, recombined in a “dream” pass, and narrated back as revised understanding.

Python prototypes from 2025 · Next: measuring forgetting

Sphota

Does the sound of a word carry part of its meaning?

Inspired by Bhartṛhari’s sphoṭa and the Vedic oral tradition. A test, using today’s audio analysis, of whether any sound–meaning patterns hold across languages with no shared history.

Research design stage · Next: a cross-language word test

How we work

Both projects start from ideas, some drawn from Indian philosophy, and try to turn them into questions that experiments can answer. We name the existing research each question depends on, state what result would count against us, and plan to publish negative results as well as positive ones.

The projects are separate: each has its own questions, methods and code, and progress in one does not depend on the other.
