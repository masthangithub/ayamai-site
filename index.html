Two open questions about how machines might come to understand.

AYAMAI, pronounced “I am AI”, hosts two independent research projects, each at an early stage. We share the ideas, the code and the doubts, to find researchers who want to explore them with us.

DREAMS

Can a machine keep learning without forgetting what it knew?

Data Relations, Entropy, And Mind Simulations. A study of continual learning and reflective loops, in which experiences are encoded as data, linked into memory, recombined in a “dream” pass and narrated back as revised understanding.

Sphota

Does the sound of a word carry part of its meaning?

Inspired by Bhartṛhari’s sphoṭa and the Vedic oral tradition. A test, using today’s audio analysis, of whether any sound–meaning patterns hold across languages with no shared history.

How we work

Both projects start from ideas, some drawn from Indian philosophy, and turn them into questions that experiments can answer. We name the research each question depends on, state what result would count against us, and publish negative results as well as positive ones.

The projects are separate. Each has its own questions, methods and code, and progress in one does not depend on the other. Read the original vision note to see where both began.
